Title:Drumset Instruments
Crossword Size:13
BassDrum:The large drum that produces the low booming sound, played with a foot pedal.
SnareDrum:The central drum that produces a sharp, staccato sound, played with drumsticks.
HiHat:A pair of cymbals mounted on a stand, played with a foot pedal and drumsticks.
TomTom:A cylindrical drum that produces a resonant sound, used in various sizes.
FloorTom:A larger tom-tom that stands on the floor, producing a deeper sound.
CrashCymbal:A cymbal used for accenting, producing a loud, crashing sound.
RideCymbal:A larger cymbal used for steady rhythms, producing a clear, sustained sound.
SplashCymbal:A small cymbal used for quick accents, producing a short, splashy sound.
ChinaCymbal:A cymbal with a unique, trashy sound, often used for special effects.
Cowbell:A metal percussion instrument producing a distinct, clanging sound.
